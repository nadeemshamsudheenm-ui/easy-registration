Seamless Semester Registration & Instant Approvals

A unified, end-to-end digital portal connecting Students, Accounts, Transport, Laboratory Assistants, and Heads of Departments for swift semester clearance and enrollment.

Access Portal Login → New Student Sign Up
📝

1. Student Self-Service

Submit semester registration forms, upload payment details, declare college transport requirements, and monitor real-time approval stages.

💳

2. Accounts Clearance

Accounts department verifies tuition fees, receipt numbers, and payment dates before forwarding applications automatically to the next desk.

🚌

3. Transport Clearance

Transport officer verifies bus route allocation and fee status for students availing college transportation. Automatically bypassed if not opted!

🔬

4. Laboratory Clearance

Lab assistants verify hardware equipment return, lab manual records, and issue "No Dues" clearance with a single click.

👨‍🏫

5. HOD Final Approval

Head of Department reviews student CGPA, backlogs, and all prerequisite clearance stamps before granting final enrollment sign-off.

🖨️

6. Official Clearance Card

Upon HOD approval, students can immediately print or download the official, stamp-verified Semester Registration Slip with barcode.
